Meet the Band

Mary Faith, Doy
& Bill.

The members featured here — with photos and bios provided by the band.

Lead Vocals

VocalsMary Faith Blanco

Mary Faith Blanco is a powerhouse vocalist and seasoned performer whose musical journey began at age six on Philippine television. A champion of numerous singing competitions, she has performed for prime ministers, heads of state, and distinguished audiences, from elegant lounges to major concerts.

Now based in Florida, Mary Faith has performed at Skipper’s Cove, Sailor’s Return, E.R. Bradley’s, Hutchinson Island Bar & Grill, Pickled Bar & Restaurant, and numerous private events. Her repertoire spans 70s & 80s classics, dance favorites, powerful ballads, and popular hits across generations, delivered with elegance, energy, and commanding stage presence.

A Doctor of Dental Medicine and Army Reservist in the Philippines, Mary Faith brings discipline, professionalism, and passion to every performance — creating a polished and unforgettable live-music experience.

Keys

KeyboardsDoy Dela Rosa

Doy is a seasoned, self-taught keyboardist whose musical journey began at the age of 10. With a natural ear for music and a passion for rhythm, he developed his craft through years of playing and performing, with a particular love for dance music and infectious grooves.

His musical influences include iconic electronic and alternative bands such as Depeche Mode, New Order, and The Cure, bringing a distinctive blend of melody, atmosphere, and rhythm to his playing.

Throughout his career, Doy has performed with numerous bands, including Brown Sugar Band, The Lee Riders, The Black Pearl Band, A Touch of Gray, JUZT UZ Band, Night Groove Band, Music Machine, and FLIP ZYDE Band. Today, Doy brings his experience, versatility, and love of live music to Retrospect.

Drums

DrumsBill “Willy Vil”

Bill is a seasoned drummer with a musical career spanning more than five decades. He began playing drums in the mid-1960s, developing his craft through years of performing a wide range of styles, including rock & roll, classic rock, Motown, R&B, and Top 40 hits.

His musical journey began in the Philippines, where he performed with groups including The Counterpoints, before continuing his career in the United States with various bands, including The Stratos and FLIP ZYDE Band.

Bill was also a member of our previous band, which has evolved into RETROSPECT. With decades of experience, a strong sense of rhythm, and an unmistakable passion for live music, Bill brings the energy, versatility, and steady groove that keep every RETROSPECT performance moving.
